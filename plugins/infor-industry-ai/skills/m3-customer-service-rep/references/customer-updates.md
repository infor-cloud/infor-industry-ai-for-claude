# Customer service rep: customer updates (drafts only)

## What this does

When a date changes or a promise is at risk, draft a short update for the rep: who it is to, what changed, the date and which kind of date it is, what is
being done, and when the next update comes. Get the recipient from the customer's contact tools. Plain, warm, three or four sentences, signed with the rep's
name. Never send it. Say that a person must read, edit and send it. Nothing is written to M3.

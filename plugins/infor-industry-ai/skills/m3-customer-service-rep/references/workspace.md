# Customer service rep: the workspace (fixed dashboard)

## What this does

The rep's fixed workspace is the artifact **Act & Track — CS Rep** (bundled as `assets/rep-dashboard.html`; the rep's own link wins; see `m3/page-style.md`). It shows **only real M3 data**: no sample data. If M3 cannot be reached it shows a clear message,
never made-up data. It has four tabs.
- **In every list** the customer's own reference is shown when you hover over the order number, and the search box finds an order by number, customer, item or reference.
- **Responsible user**: the lists show your own orders. The dropdown holds you and your colleagues from the same company. To look at someone else's orders, type part of their name or user ID in the search box next to it and press Find (or Enter): one match switches to that person at once, several matches replace the dropdown list until you empty the box. M3 hands out its user list 30 users at a time, so the page reads the whole list once (a few calls, in the background) and searches it itself.
- **Needs Action**: orders where something is wrong, sorted by the earliest confirmed delivery date (click any column heading to sort by it; click again to reverse), with chips for each group (credit hand-overs, orders the rep can fix, orders with no
  fix). Dates are shown with an icon and a colour only, no words: red with an icon for a date that has passed, amber for due today or a confirmed date later
  than requested.
- **On Track**: orders going as planned, by requested date.
- **Focus Orders**: the open orders of the focus order type, each on its fulfilment path (make, buy, transfer, credit, stock). Click a row to unfold the same recommended options as in Needs Action, under the list. Each row shows the order's status exactly as Needs Action does (icon and word, on the right) and a line under the bar that starts with **Your move:** when the rep has to act, otherwise with **Next:** and a sentence that names who acts (the planner, the buyer, the supplier, the warehouse). There is no separate label or button on the row: the buttons are in the options below. The bar is built from facts: **Ordered**, then the supply steps of that path, then **Allocated**, **Picking** and **Delivered**. A step is filled when M3 data says so: every line allocated (allocated quantity), a picking list exists (the quantity on picking lists covers every line), a purchase order's step from M3's own purchase order statuses (11 to 14 stopped, 20 printed, 31 to 35 confirmed by the supplier, 40 and 45 on the way, 50 and up received), a production order's step from the quantities made and received. The open circle is the first step that is not true yet: the same thing the line under the bar talks about. The last step is never filled while the order is in the list, because a delivered order leaves it. If M3 cannot say whether a picking list exists, "Picking" is not claimed.
- **Create CO**: search the customer and items, order details, material lines with free stock and earliest date, then create (`rush-order.md`, "Creating the order"). Defaults: the focus order type from Settings (one field: it shows the description from M3, click it to pick another type or type a code; a code that does not exist is shown in red and blocks Create), requested delivery on the next working day (Friday, Saturday and Sunday give Monday; holidays are not considered), priority High, you as the responsible user (change it with the same "Find other sales reps" search as on Needs Action). There is no order date field: the order date is today. A line that cannot be delivered by its requested date shows the options that work before the order exists, in the same style as the Actions list (use an alternative item, change the requested date; the one with the earliest date is highlighted). After the order is created the lists are reloaded for its responsible user and the page opens the new order under Needs Action or On Track, where all the options are offered (take stock from another order, transfer, alert the buyer or planner). The confirmation and the result use plain words and no M3 technical terms.

**Six key measures** (click one to filter the list): orders at risk; past due orders; upcoming orders due in 7 days; **due within 2 days and not picked**
(allocated lines with no picking list: credit-stopped ones are obvious, the others get a "Remind the warehouse" action); orders stopped for credit;
**backorder value** (past-due quantity with nothing allocated, times the price; click it for the top backordered items with an "Alert buyer" button; a daily
history kept in the page's database gives the trend).

**The selected order** shows **Recommended options** (the heading of the box; under it one sentence per line with the days late and the date the plan supports), then the groups **Options** and **Escalate internally**, and, collapsed, **Supply details**, **Incoming transfers** and **Update customer**.

**Transfer from another warehouse** has three states, read from the M3 material plan (so it looks the same whoever opens the order, and does not depend on the page's own log): **Available** with the Create proposal button when no firm transfer covers the line; **Requested**, with no button, when a firm distribution order proposal (status 20) or a released distribution order covers the line, with its number, quantity, source warehouse, arrival date and status, and a pointer to Escalate internally to ask the planner to bring it forward; and still Available, with a warning, when only M3's own loose proposal (status 10, which can disappear at the next planning run) exists, or when a firm proposal is already coming but other orders use it. Supply details names the kind of each transfer (M3 planning, status 10 / firm proposal, status 20 / distribution order).

**Incoming transfers** is a closed fold-down after Supply details. It lists every transfer in the M3 plan on its way to the warehouse of the short lines, also proposals other people made: item, transfer number, from → to, quantity, arrival date, status in plain words ("Proposed by M3 planning" with a note that it can disappear at the next planning run, "Proposal waiting for the planner", "Released distribution order", plus M3's suggested earlier date when it has one), and whether this order uses it. It shows when there is at least one such transfer or the item is distributed.

**Options** are things the rep does in M3, in this order: allocate from free stock, transfer from another warehouse, take stock from another order (from orders that are due later than this one and more than 3 days from today, whatever their order type), swap to an alternative item, change the confirmed date. Each option shows Available or Not available with the reason, the new date it gives (a real date, today's date for options that work from free stock, with how many days late), and its own button. The action that gives the **earliest delivery date** has its date highlighted in green (on a tie the swap wins, because it takes nothing from another customer). **Escalate internally** is the buyer or planner: **Email** opens an email draft to them (address read from M3), with the same text the alert used to have. The page no longer sends M3 application messages to the buyer or planner. The one exception is **Remind**, for an order that only waits for the picking list: it still sends an application message to the warehouse. Email only opens a draft in the rep's own mail program; the page never sends email.

**Resolution Log** at the bottom of Needs Action: one line for everything the rep decided or did (alerts, reminders, stock taken, swaps, proposals,
allocations, created orders, customer updates marked as sent), with the time and the rep's name, kept in the page's database.

Every write asks for confirmation, reads the result back and reloads the orders. Credit stops are never released there. The **Update customer** fold-down sits under "Supply details" in the order's card (or in the credit box, or on its own for an order without either). It is closed by default and closes again when another order is opened. Opening it shows the draft customer update for the open order, with Edit, Copy text, Email and Mark as sent; the page never sends anything to a customer. There is no separate Customer Update card and no "Draft customer update" button.

**Settings.** The page has two settings, in a **Settings** fold-down in the footer: **Days back** (the look-back, default 90) and the **focus order type**. The focus order type box is short and takes 3 characters, the length of an M3 customer order type. A notice at the top of the Focus Orders tab asks for it until it is set. Until then the Focus Orders tab stays empty and the Create CO form starts with an empty order type. The focus order type plays no part in take stock. It is remembered for the person in a private row of the page's own database (nobody else can read it; the page uses no browser storage), so it is said once; if the page cannot store it, it asks again at every visit and says "Kept for this visit only". A shared default can be set once in the row `config/settings`, and a person's own choice wins. **Clear** forgets it. The allocation origin (1) and the message type (001) are M3's usual codes, built in and not shown, and the page has no link to the credit controller: the credit hand-over only reads the credit status. The type of a donor order is read from its header and only shown in the take-stock row and pop-up; it decides nothing. A new order line is sent without a unit, so M3 uses the item's own; a swapped line keeps the unit M3 gave the old one.

**Look-back (Days back, default 90).** Needs Action, On Track, Focus Orders and the key measures only count **open orders whose requested delivery date (on the order header) is within the look-back**: due 90 days before today or later. An order due today or later is never left out. Under each list and under the key measures the page says the rule in plain words: *Looking back 90 days: open orders due since <date> and later are shown; N older open orders left out. Change this in Settings.* The date is compared by the page itself after it has read the rep's open orders (a date filter in the search can silently return nothing). Like the focus order type it is remembered per person; the value must be a whole number from 1 to 999 (the box takes 3 characters), otherwise the box says so and nothing changes; **Clear** goes back to 90. The text view in chat uses the same default of 90 days and says so (`needs-my-attention-today.md`). If the search returns a round number of rows (about 30), the list may be cut off before the look-back is applied, and the page says so. **The rule itself is written once, in `m3/order-set.md`**: the text view follows that same file, and the page is tested against its worked cases, so the two cannot drift apart without the test failing.

**Scope.** Only the rep's role is built here. The buyer and the production planner have their own use cases; the rep only alerts and hands over.

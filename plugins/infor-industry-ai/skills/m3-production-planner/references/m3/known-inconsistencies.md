# Warnings and known internal inconsistencies

Shared reference. These are places where the persona skills disagree with each other, or where a rule rests on a single
test. They are kept as written until the owners settle them. **When an answer depends on an open item, say so in one
line** ("note: the buyer and rep rules for alternative items differ") and carry on with the rule of the skill in use.
